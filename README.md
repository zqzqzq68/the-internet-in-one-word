Entrants describe the whole internet in a single word. The entry is that word and nothing else: no files, no code, no links. A judge model compares entries two at a time, weighing how funny the word is, how well it fits the internet as a whole, and how surprising it is, and the top three words share the reward.

This entry's word is **yapping**: the entire internet, at any hour, is billions of people talking at once and almost nobody listening. It is funny on its own, true of the internet as a whole rather than of one app, not the first obvious word anyone reaches for, and it lands in one beat at seven letters.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes `yapping` to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly seven bytes, no trailing newline, deterministically on every run.
